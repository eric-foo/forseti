## Success contract (bound before edits)

Goal: consume the normal policy-v2 completed case into source-backed findings,
answers, and review without putting the whole raw corpus into every request.
Existing extraction and verification remain authoritative for their own scope;
they do not establish downstream relevance or correct interpretation.

Invariants: exact complete inventory, separate support/opposition and original
conditions/roles, explicit unused/residual explanations, no silent truncation,
local content-bound reuse, original hashes and finite replay unchanged.
Acquisition, corpus repair, new consolidation, and global finite-route adoption
are outside this change.

Signals, initially `PLANNED_NOT_OBSERVED`:

- Public normal advance with a commission reaches source judgments, assembly,
  review, and a persisted answer. Split support/opposition and an important
  unused observation remain available together; harmless no-action remains
  explicit. Wrong-cause perturbation: omit an admitted unit from a valid-shaped
  response and require exact-coverage rejection.
- Every production request includes schema, context, and output reservations
  in capacity admission. An oversized row, finding slice, aggregate answer, or
  review must block before dispatch; no partial success is allowed.
- Missing, duplicate, and foreign results fail at the consumer boundary.
  Repeated valid requests reuse accepted work; evidence, checks, and answers
  invalidate only dependent work. Historical finite artifacts stay replayable.
- Structural fixtures do not establish semantic quality. A cold live case must
  discriminate contrary evidence, material omission, uncertainty, and harmless
  residual/no-action preservation, with expectations excluded from actor input.

Baseline: normal `advance` ends at `view.json`; no commissioned bounded answer
surface exists. Parent separately preserved 50 passing unchanged review-evidence
and finite-closeout-judgment tests. This is an absent-surface baseline, not a
fabricated red test.

Review checkpoint: apply the repository's conditional success-implement review
predicate. Any material boundary lacking independent evidence receives a
bounded operator-courier review prompt; no reviewer dispatch in this unit.

## Integration success contract (bound before integration edits)

One supported normal `advance` command can begin with an explicitly paired,
native-validated existing bundle and verified compilation for the same source,
retain that start across resume, run unchanged policy-v2 reconciliation, then
deliver bounded consumer jobs through `intake-judgment-job` and
`submit-judgment-job`. Complete/selected verification retains original provenance;
missing or changed originals and a mismatched source block before judgment.

New answer reviews explicitly classify repair scope and may propose exact edits
under the existing `review_evidence` policy. One admissible patch receives one
independent recheck and existing clean-only selection; no inferred classification,
extra rewrite, automatic retry, or source reread. Saved unclassified reviews
retain their failure. Publication shares staged no-replace mechanics and resumes
a valid response missing its receipt without creating a new judgment.

Bound validation is structural public-CLI replay from genuinely native-verified
synthetic inputs, established intake/submit, exact correction/recheck, no-work
resume, provenance perturbations and interrupted publication. No provider calls
or Summer Fridays processing; prior live evidence stays historically pinned,
full current-case fit remains unproven, specialized point readers stay unchanged.

## Implementation

The public normal `advance` entry now accepts an answer commission and explicit
capacity settings. It reaches source review, cross-batch assembly, independent
answer review, bounded original-source reopening, and immutable result publication.
The native reconciliation route and existing finite/replay implementations are
unchanged. The semantic-integration contract's **Bounded complete-case answer and
review** section owns the executable interface.

Existing verified meanings, residuals, and reasoned no-unit dispositions are
reused. Source review covers the claim-bearing rows and explicit check anchors;
it does not reclassify every no-unit row. Every distinct unused/no-claim reason
remains available to final actors. Exact source membership stays in immutable
manifests; compact handles and zero-based origin-attribution indices preserve
the same records without repeatedly printing long source identities. Known
equal/distinct origin identity, roles, and independence posture cross batches;
unknown identity is not minted into a new person. The established worker intake delivers only the measured prompt/schema envelope;
required context is already present once, and the duplicated local-validation
payload is not redelivered.

Content-addressed requests preserve accepted work, reject changed accepted
responses, and stop on a missing response whose acceptance receipt exists.
Changing checks reuses unchanged source judgments/assembly. Changing a source
row invalidates its slice and downstream consumers. Reviews bind actual answers.
New reviews explicitly classify findings/check scope and may supply hash-bound
exact answer repairs. Existing `review_evidence` application, composition and
clean-only selection connect one repair to one independent recheck. Inadmissible,
upstream-only, unclassified and failed corrections preserve the original and
visible failure. This does not claim the optional finite v4 improvement rule.
Unchanged source jobs and answers remain exact; no extra rewrite or retry runs.
