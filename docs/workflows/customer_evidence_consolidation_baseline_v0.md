## Baseline method

1. **Bind the existing evidence and question once.** Reuse complete captured
   source bodies, stable references, parent/product context, speaker and source
   roles, relevant native attributes and known origin identities. Preserve
   unknowns and original bytes. Mechanical identity/coverage checks belong in
   code; they do not certify meaning. Refresh the binding when its inputs or
   target change, not at every conversational turn.
2. **Use a cold Sol/medium author for source reading and notes.** Read all
   admitted records in complete, practical batches that fit both model capacity
   and the actual tool display. Save compact, source-linked notes retaining
   consequential support, opposition, conditions and behavior. Completed notes
   survive interruptions; resume unfinished work without rereading completed
   raw batches by default. One author session can involve many billed model
   operations. Indefinite compaction is not a cost-saving guarantee.
3. **Compose one report in a fresh context from all completed notes.** Reconcile
   the notes against the commissioned question and preserve consequential qualifications or
   explain consequential exclusions in the existing limitations prose. Reopen
   originals only where necessary to resolve a claim. Use code for reference
   mapping, counts and derived editions. A separate label for every row and a
   hierarchy of merged propositions are not prerequisites for this report.
   Carry the question, applicable semantic rules, notes and source-reference
   map, without the reading conversation or earlier report. Prefer a single
   response saved and mapped by local code. If all notes and the required answer
   cannot fit, expose the capacity limit and use only the additional synthesis
   needed to reconcile them; never silently drop notes. The measured smaller
   final-writing token count has not established equivalent dollar savings or
   a latency improvement.
4. **Run one Claude source review after the report is complete.** The reviewer
   proposes corrections and has no editing rights. Review the exact answer,
   its cited originals, all batch notes for lost themes, and a small declared
   source-family-stratified sample of uncited originals. Resolve suspected
   omissions against their original context. Disclose actual coverage; the
   sample does not establish exhaustive recall. Follow the existing
   operator-courier rules when commissioning the reviewer.
5. **Home adjudicates and applies narrow corrections.** Inspect contested
   sources and their context, accept/modify/reject the proposals, and judge
   materiality against the whole commissioned question. Ordinary paraphrases
   and extra examples do not automatically justify reopening the report.
   Preserve the submitted original and write the corrected consumer separately.
   Do not repeat a full same-family review or relaunch the author for local
   corrections that can be closed from the cited evidence.
6. **Verify the saved consumer and finish.** Check reference resolution,
   identity/count arithmetic, source preservation and actual file delivery.
   Report unresolved material issues honestly. Extra review is driven by a
   specific unresolved issue, not by a requirement to review every turn or
   review a review. Update the existing current-result pointer and retire the
   courier without creating a new standing ledger or approval stage.

The [claim-support contract](../../forseti/product/spines/judgment/claim_support/forseti_intelligence_claim_support_contract_v0.md)
and [source-label materiality rule](../../.agents/workflow-overlay/review-lanes.md#source-label-materiality)

## Lower-cost execution target

The target is to pay once for interpreting each admitted source body, once for
reconciling the resulting notes, and once for the external review. Necessary
parent context, source checking and contested-claim repair remain real work.
The aim is bounded repeated input, not the smallest possible call count or a
body-only token floor. Review sampling does not authorize sampling the author's
admitted corpus.

Shared body/context text appears once per unit with explicit references. | `customer_evidence_report.py prepare` is implemented and locally verified on all 6,908 admitted Dieux records. |

Batch size follows complete-context, output and actual delivery fit. Start from
the proven packer and measured working sizes; enlarge only when that removes
repeated overhead without harming reading or note retention. Keep a thread or
other context-dependent unit together where practical; when split, attach the
required parent/product context explicitly. Do not repeat the whole global
context dictionary in every batch. Do not split into tiny batches or rely on
autocompaction merely to claim one continuing session. Parallel execution is
optional for latency and does not by itself save tokens.

Fresh readers must share the commissioned question and source-backed product
identity map. Notes retain consequential support, opposition, conditions,
behavior, minority findings and unresolved interpretations with source refs.
The final writer reconciles across all notes; it may reopen original context
for a specific ambiguity. This is the quality tradeoff to test: isolated reads
remove growing history but cannot rely on another batch's remembered detail.
Do not compensate by reinstating per-row labels, generic review passes or
model-authored bookkeeping.

## Installed report helper

Run `.agents/tools/customer_evidence_report.py --help` for the operator interface;
its focused offline tests are `--selftest`. The helper requires the existing
harness dependencies, including a locally cached `o200k_base` tokenizer. It uses
`forseti-harness/runners/run_codex_provider_attempt.py` for native execution and
the maintained receipt, profile and event validators for acceptance. It adds no
model client, reviewer launcher, checked-report state or packet promotion.

The input is a frozen JSON envelope with `original_rows`, `original_context` and
`original_containers`, as in the existing Dieux `selected-originals.json`. Rows
retain native evidence IDs, complete text, source refs, actor/origin keys, dates,
roles, engagement, parent/product context refs, container keys and any native
metadata. Admission is the supplied row list; this helper does not select or
exclude records. Context and containers must resolve. Optional `--source-bindings`
checks an existing `source_files` map and optional `batches` map of file hashes.
The question file, original corpus and supplied bindings are frozen per run.

From the repository root, preparation is offline:

```powershell
python -B -X utf8 .agents/tools/customer_evidence_report.py prepare --corpus CORPUS.json --question-file QUESTION.txt --run-dir NEW_RUN_DIRECTORY
```

Successful `prepare` returns the helper's full saved-request check. Successful
`accept` rechecks saved requests and outputs under the run lock; `run` checks
state before each launch and accepts completed responses. Do not add a
standalone full `check` after an unchanged,
successful operation; use it when inputs or saved files change, an operation
fails or is interrupted, or an independent consumer needs a fresh audit. This
avoids repeated whole-run reconstruction without removing launch, stale-file
or acceptance guards.

Under the existing run authorization, `run --run-dir RUN_DIRECTORY
--max-requests N` executes at most N new requests and resumes exact completed
work. Use `--codex-executable ABSOLUTE_NATIVE_EXE` when native selection needs an
override; the maintained runner accepts a native executable, not `codex.cmd`.
It retains the existing executable/profile/authentication checks. Preparation,
`compose`, `accept` and `check` make no model calls. `accept --run-dir
RUN_DIRECTORY --request read-001 --attempt-dir COMPLETED_NATIVE_ATTEMPT` can
recover the native-completed/save-interrupted boundary without rereading sources.
Use request `synthesis` for an already completed final-writing attempt. Failed
or unknown attempts remain inspectable and do not trigger automatic retries.

Each reading request contains only its assigned material source fields, complete
referenced context/container content, the question, semantic rules and
source-backed product identities. Complete conversations stay together where capacity permits;
an oversized conversation splits at record boundaries with required context
reattached. Literal body/context text and identical context/capture definitions
share storage while distinct native records remain distinct. New reading
requests also share repeated `native_product`, `native_author` and
`accounting_reason` values inside that request when the full encoded input is
smaller. A version-bound dictionary carries each complete native value;
explicit tagged references resolve before reading and preserve every original
`/record/...` citation pointer. A literal matching a reference marker is
escaped, so no native value is reinterpreted. Saved runs without this version
keep their original prompt bytes and reconstruction. Short references
replace technical evidence IDs, source locators and artifact IDs in model input;
those audit fields and every original edge remain in the frozen corpus and are
restored locally. The exclusion list is explicit and narrow: all unfamiliar
fields and all native retailer metadata still reach the reader. Coverage is a compiler check, not a model-authored label
or declaration for every row. Request bytes, exact reconstruction, native receipt
bindings and output bytes are rechecked on resumption.

New runs freeze `paragraph_evidence_v2`. Readers return `paragraphs`, each with
finished Markdown `text` and its `evidence` array. Each evidence selection contains
`handle`, `owner`, literal `quote` and semantic `role`. A body uses the T identifier
already beside its text, with empty `owner` unless `ambiguous_text_owners` lists
alternatives. Only genuinely ambiguous text carries an owner list in new input.
Shared text with distinct or unknown owners requires an exact listed owner/pointer;
code never picks a person from matching words. Context text follows the same rule.
When one assigned body also appears as parent context, code resolves that single
original only if every original parent edge carries its exact source locator and
full body text, and the locator uniquely identifies that native row. Product edges,
missing/different locators and multiple bodies remain ambiguous. Explicit context
selections keep their context role. The earlier full-owner-list input remains
reconstructible. Other native
fields use `Rnnnn/record/...` or `Rnnnn/context/Cn/...` as the handle and an
empty owner. For a string field, `quote` is a nonempty literal substring with
the existing source-bound ASCII-case restoration. For a native finite JSON number
or boolean, `quote` is the entire canonical JSON value (`14`, `1.0`, `true`,
`false`); partial, coerced or wrong-field values fail. Missing, null, list,
object and nonfinite values cannot be cited. No separate prose R citations or
parallel citation list is authored.
Code compiles native source bindings, speaker categories and rendered references.
Customer bodies used as background remain customer speech; parent/product and
other metadata remain context; retailer reply fields remain retailer speech.

Composition receives all compiled notes and a code-issued `evidence_catalog`.
The writer returns the same paragraph shape with E-handle strings as evidence;
code copies the accepted literal citation rather than asking the writer to
repeat its tuple. The saved `paragraphs.json` binds each paragraph to its citations
and customer observation/known-origin counts. Unknown origins remain explicit;
known identity keys do not certify independent people. `source-bindings.json`
maps originals and actors to paragraph uses, exposing repeated use of one original.
Its identity fields describe the carrying record and apply only to `paragraphs`,
where that record's customer body is cited. Context or retailer uses of the same
record are listed separately as `non_customer_paragraphs`, present only when they
exist, and do not inherit that customer's identity or origin credit.
Two excerpts from one customer original count as one native observation; equal
text from different records does not collapse their identities. Context and
retailer speech do not add customer observations. These are mechanical source
facts, not automatic corroboration, entailment or causal judgments.

Runs without the frozen v2 marker retain their original Markdown-plus-citations
contract and exact reconstruction. Their accepted notes, explicit corrections,
case restorations and failures remain reusable without regeneration or migration.
Legacy continuation retains its frozen contract; a new contract needs a new run.

In legacy runs every prose reference needs a validated citation. Additional source-valid
citations may preserve context without appearing in the prose; they do not
establish an additional finding or person. A quotation differing only in ASCII
letter case can be restored from its bound source when there is exactly one
matching span. Accepted citations then contain the literal source bytes and
the acceptance record lists the restoration; the native response stays unchanged.
The same exact whole-value rule admits finite native numbers and booleans at
their bound pointer while retaining `quote` as a string.
Ambiguous matches, changed wording, punctuation or whitespace, missing prose
citations and wrong source/speaker bindings still fail without automatic reruns.

An occasional verified defect in an unaccepted reading note can be corrected
locally with `accept ... --home-correction FILE`. The JSON file contains
`native_response_sha256`, a source-backed `rationale`, and the complete corrected
`response`. Check the original record and relevant parent context before changing
the prose or citation; matching words alone do not establish the same speaker.
The helper preserves the native response and failure history, pins the separate
`home-correction.json`, and runs the same source checks on the derivative before
using it in synthesis. This is home adjudication, not a clean native result or
automatic source reassignment. Already accepted notes and final reports cannot
be revised through this option. Keep the current model/input method for an
isolated defect; reassess if the same error recurs rather than accumulating
manual fixes or adding a standing rereview of every note.

For v2, a correction that changes a paragraph's linked handles/owners must also
revise that paragraph's prose explicitly. Paragraphs are matched by their native
text, not position, so inserting or dropping paragraphs does not exempt unchanged
prose. A source-only repair is rejected with
the paragraph and before/after evidence selections; the helper cannot silently
retain a false distinct-speaker or product assertion. Changed prose still needs
source-backed home adjudication: this guard does not certify arbitrary wording.
Wrong handles, ambiguous owners and nonliteral quotations fail without source
rebinding, automatic retries or a paid per-batch reviewer.

`compose` includes every accepted note in one fresh request. Its compact table
preserves native actor/origin equality, source role/venue and product identity;
literal IDs and locators remain in the frozen corpus and are mapped locally.
Different cross-venue identities still do not prove different people. The writer
must use notes for substantive claims, consequential dates and engagement; the
identity table supplies no missing source assertion. All-notes overflow stops
before generation. It neither drops notes nor automatically builds a synthesis
hierarchy. Any necessary additional synthesis remains an explicit bounded next
move under this baseline.

Saved consumers are `synthesis/report.md`, `report-originals.md`, `citations.json`
and `cited-originals.json`, beside the unchanged native response and acceptance
binding. Notes, attempts, usage and failure logs remain under each request.
`REPORT_SAVED_UNREVIEWED` describes delivery only. The baseline's one external
review and home adjudication still follow; this helper does not launch them or
overwrite the submitted report for corrections. Source/question/request changes,
changed accepted notes or changed report files invalidate reuse. New output
directories and exact-byte continuation preserve existing runs.

The defaults are a 45,000-token complete reading-request budget, a conservative
180,000-token effective context budget, 12,000 reading-output and 24,000
synthesis-output/reasoning reserves, 4,000 other-overhead tokens and a 2,000,000-byte
stdin-input limit. Whole-request measurement includes the schema and the native
runner's additional instruction. These are configurable local budgets, not
observed provider capacity or provider-enforced output caps. Oversized complete
records and all-notes requests fail visibly; native output over the reserved
budget is rejected on acceptance. No tool-display excerpt is used for delivery.
