## Purpose and ownership

Choose sources that can answer the actual question, preserve their evidence,
and explain the resulting choice without turning reputation into proof.
A reader should be able to reconstruct why a source was selected, what it
supports, what remains unknown, and what would change the recommendation.

This is the reusable operator guide requested on 2026-10-05. It combines the
method used in the RaidRelay investigation with the owner's subsequent
corrections. It is **not a claim that every step was performed in the original
run**. The trial record remains the history; this guide describes the improved
method. It applies existing owners: CSB commissions the question, Scanning
discovers, Capture preserves, Cleaning reconciles, Judgment evaluates, and
Deliver presents. The linked contracts govern those boundaries.

## 1. Define what a useful answer must accomplish

State the outcome, user, conditions and tradeoff before searching. For RaidRelay:
fresh Global Asmodian character, a general route through level 45, worthwhile
power-ups and collections along the MSQ, and less later backtracking. Pure
leveling speed is only one part of that outcome. Mark class exceptions and
unknown build compatibility instead of merging them into the general route.

Split the question into the claims that could change the answer: which steps
exist, what they award, when they unlock, whether the route reaches them,
whether the diversion is worthwhile, and whether a newcomer can follow it.
Different evidence answers each question. Do not make one overall credibility
score for the creator or the entire guide.

## 2. Choose where to look

Use the CSB Source-Family Map and the existing evidence inventory. Reuse relevant
captures after checking their freshness and gaps. Choose each next source for
the information it could add, including a contradiction; do not fill a quota
of platforms or sources.

| Information needed | Useful place to look |
| --- | --- |
| Current rules, changes and unlock conditions | Publisher material and version-matched records; inspect a fan database's provenance rather than calling it official. |
| A practical sequence or demonstrated result | Detailed creator guides, maps, recordings, tests and run logs. |
| Confusing steps, workarounds and actual use | Relevant forums, comments, reviews and optionally Discord conversations. |
| Whether an article opportunity has search demand | SERP and search-interest evidence; community questions supply language and candidate topics. |

Discord is an optional community research choice at cycle intake. Select a
server for its topic fit, accessible relevant discussions and substantive
answers, not member count alone. A public search can discover server leads;
an invite or search snippet does not establish internal quality or coverage.
The capture route and current limits are in the Armory's **Discord — community
research and SEO discovery** section.

## 3. Discover candidates and preserve the discovery trail

Search the outcome plus the material conditions. Follow useful citations and
creator references back to their originals. Search for corrections, failures,
alternative routes and update notes as well as endorsements. Search-result
rank, followers, badges and polished maps can help nominate a source; none
establishes accuracy or optimality.

For meaningful discovery moves, keep the actual query, venue/referrer, filters,
date, candidate URL and selection reason in the existing run record. Distinguish
the first discovery from later confirmation. If the original route to a source
was not recorded, say so; do not reconstruct it from plausible browsing history.

Screen candidates for task fit, inspectable evidence, version/date, relevant
demonstrated experience, coverage of the needed step, and known conflicts.
Unknown authors remain eligible. A novice is competent to report their own
difficulty even when their explanation of its cause is unverified.

## 4. Capture the evidence that matters

Open the original source before using it as evidence. Capture the relevant
passage, conversation window, measurement or video segment, with the existing
packet's provenance and limitations. Preserve the native actor, role, dates,
version, location and engagement, with explicit unavailability where needed.
Separate source statements from researcher interpretation.

For Discord, use a bounded server/channel/date/topic search, then open useful
hits with their question, replies and corrections. Preserve message permalinks
and IDs when exposed; a search-result extract alone can hide quoted parents or
the answer's conditions. Follow the Armory access route; a blocked login,
hidden channel or incomplete history is an access/coverage gap, not zero demand.

For screenshots or maps, retain the original and its source reference. A useful
crop can remove browser chrome and empty space, but must retain the evidence's
meaning and a recoverable connection to the original. Record the timestamp or
message ID. Internal proof images need not clutter the reader-facing guide.
Map proximity alone does not prove a walkable shortcut, pickup access or order.

For a performance claim, capture the relevant start and finish, visible timer
and its meaning, completion outcome, character/loadout, region/build and run
rules. Check relevant cuts, pauses or omitted stages. A displayed timer is
valuable direct evidence; it is not automatically elapsed wall time. Sampled
frames can confirm a local action without establishing the whole run's duration
or continuity. Collect more only when the proposed claim needs it.

## 5. Evaluate what each item actually supports

Apply the claim-support contract to each bounded proposition. These evidence
types answer different questions; this table is not a universal ranking.

| Evidence | What it strengthens | What it does not establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Version-matched quest/database record | Quest name, prerequisite, objective or reward as recorded by that source. | Whether a route is efficient, easy to follow or worth its detours. |
| Creator's own inspectable timed recording | Demonstrated actions, result and timing under the observed conditions. Authorship does not reduce it to mere promotion. | Universal optimality, ordinary-player repeatability or a fair comparison to an unlike run. |
| Detailed route explanation or map | The proposed sequence and the author's stated reasons; practical suitability for a companion. | Proof that every step was executed, all rewards collected, or every bend is mandatory. |
| Generic praise, likes or agreeing reactions | Audience approval or resonance, with native metric/context retained. | Successful use, factual accuracy, efficiency or independent experience counts. |
| Specific firsthand use report | That person's reported result, difficulty or benefit under stated conditions. | A representative success rate or a measured result beyond the report. |
| Independent comparable runs or detailed reports | Recurrence of the same result; comparative efficiency when starting conditions and outcomes match. | Causation or performance outside those conditions. |
| Specific correction, failure or counterexample | A limitation, conflict or condition that may change the recommendation. | Universal failure if the example's scope is narrower. |

Praise becomes more informative when it says what was tried and what happened:
“I followed it and obtained the rune without returning” supports a reported
benefit more than “great guide.” Do not require praise before accepting a
directly observed result, and do not let popularity override a clear defect.

Check independence for the particular claim. A guide, its author's video and
a Discord repost can have one origin while supplying different useful
observations. Preserve those observations; do not invent three independent
endorsements. Different venues alone do not satisfy the contract's cross-venue
posture, which requires independent origins in competent source roles.
Unknown account overlap remains unknown. Relevant affiliations and commercial
interests are context, not automatic disqualification.

Record credible opposition and unresolved version conflicts alongside support.
Do not settle an objective fact by majority vote or transfer confidence from
one verified quest to the rest of a guide.

## 6. Select a reference and bound the recommendation

Choose the reference best fitted to the intended artifact and support each
load-bearing recommendation separately. A useful route reference may combine
clear sequencing, relevant scope, demonstrated execution and adequate evidence
for the particular stops being recommended. It need not be proven globally
optimal to support an attributed, source-recommended companion.

Explain the choice in the existing decision record: selected source and purpose,
decisive evidence, alternatives actually considered and why not selected,
unresolved limits, and the evidence that would change the choice. Do not imply
a comprehensive creator comparison when none was performed. A primary reference
is a working basis for this scope; its other maps and claims still require the
checks their intended use demands.

Stop or redirect collection under Scanning's materiality rules: when a further
read cannot materially change the answer, it need not be captured. A material
unanswered issue stays a named gap. Record the chosen limit rather than calling
an access-limited or budget-limited search exhaustive.

## 7. Consolidate before writing the guide

Keep source roles intact: publisher, database, guide author, firsthand user,
questioner, repost and researcher observation are different. Link duplicates
and shared origins, reconcile versions and preserve conflicting advice.
Keep a compact accepted-claim set with evidence references, allowed wording and
remaining limits, using the existing consolidation and claim-support shapes.

The presentation consumes that set. For RaidRelay, group ordinary story steps
under “follow the MSQ”; show extra stops as **do what**, adding **why** when
there is a useful power-up, unlock or avoided return trip. Attribute the route
and added analysis. Do not relabel a creator's route as independently authored
or convert suggested nearby pickups into verified yield.

## 8. Improve the method from the run record

Reuse the existing activity/decision record and capture receipts. For meaningful
moves, retain query/scope, source reference, action/failure, retained or rejected
result and reason, and measured time/actions when available. Missing timing
stays missing. This costs a short entry per meaningful search or decision, not
a new form for every click; it catches repeated searching, missing context and
unexplained source promotion.

At closeout, identify what changed the answer, what caused wasted work and what
important evidence was missed or corrected. Try a bounded improvement on a
comparable slice, then a fresh case. Judge correctness, preserved context and
important omissions before speed. More rows, praise or platforms do not mean
better research. No standing author registry, dashboard or compulsory extra
review is introduced by this guide.
